Season & Ocean Logic + Local Etiquette

The stuff that makes the difference between a good trip and a great one.

📅 Best Time to Go

  • April–May or September–October — perfect 80°F weather, warm swimming water, lower prices, manageable parking.
  • These windows dodge both the winter swells and the summer crowds.

🌊 The Winter Swim Warning

  • If you travel November–February, North Shore waves swell to dangerous 30-foot heights.
  • It's incredible for watching pro surf competitions from the safety of the sand.
  • Save actual swimming for calm city beaches or protected bays.
Never turn your back on the ocean — winter shorebreak is stronger than it looks.

🧴 Protect the Reef

  • Hawaii law strictly bans chemical sunscreens.
  • Pack or buy only mineral-based, reef-safe sunscreen (zinc oxide or titanium dioxide).
  • Rash guards cut down how much sunscreen you need at all.

👟 Trail Gear

  • Bring sturdy, older sneakers you don't mind getting stained.
  • The island's rich volcanic mud will ruin bright white shoes instantly.
  • Judd Trail is mud-packed year-round — plan for it.

🙏 Respect the Land

  • Always stay on marked paths to avoid stepping on sacred historical sites (kapu).
  • Never approach or touch marine wildlife — including honu (green sea turtles).
  • Keep a respectful distance on the sand and in the water.
